One function, two prints

Add one more input, t, and the same kind of network learns two pictures at once: the Great Wave at t = 0, Red Fuji at t = 1. It never sees anything in between, so whatever it paints there is its own guess.

WAVE is the wave, FUJI is the mountain, anything else lands in between. The network doesn't read the word: a three-line rule turns it into the number t.
